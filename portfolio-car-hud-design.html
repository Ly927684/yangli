In-Vehicle HUD & AR-HUD Design

(04.2017--06.2017)

This program is a demonstration of HUD (head up display) and AR-HUD (augmented reality head-up display) in virtual driving scenarios. The HUD and AR-HUD are Human Machine Interface of Advanced Driving and Assistance System (ADAS) to improve driving safety. There are four functions available that aid in the following maneuvers: route navigation, lane-departure warning (LDW), adaptive cruise control (ACC) and forward collision warning (FCW).

We made objects (roads, vehicles, pedestrian, indicators, architectures and bus station) in 3D Max to build driving scenarios and then add design elements in Ae.

The HUD shows the basic information, while the AR-HUD display reflects information appears to be part of the driving situation itself and move virtual information directly into the driver’s line of sight. Both the AR-HUD and HUD change synchronous.

Due to corporate rights, the whole demo is hidden.

Route Navigation

AR-HUD: 3D pyramid arrows

HUD: Partial map with navigation arrow;

Name of the current street

Forward Collision Warning (FCW)

Two levels warning

Level1: If there is a potential collision with vehicle in front, warning signal turns to yellow;

Level2: In urgent case of potential collision, warning signal turns to red.

Warning signals look like walls which stand for obstructions.

Pedestrian Forward Collision Warning (PFCW)

Two levels warning

Level1: If there is a potential collision with pedestrians in front, warning signal turns to yellow;

Level2: In urgent case of potential collision, warning signal turns to red.

Warning signals look like walls which stand for obstructions.

Land Departure Warning (LDW)

Two levels warning

Level1: If the distance of left-front wheel and lane line is less than 30cm, line turns to yellow;

Level2: If the left-front wheel is on the line or move out of its lane, line turns to red.

Raised line looks like bricks which stands for obstruction.

Team: Fang You, Yang Li, Cuiqiong Cheng, Yujia Wang, Haoran Zhou
