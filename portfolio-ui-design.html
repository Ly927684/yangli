Infinitus App (07.2016--11.2016)

Infinitus is an enterprise specializing in R&D, production and sale of Chinese herbal health products. In this project, I mainly do mobile UI design and in charge of the visual standard. There are 8 models, each model has a main color to help users recognize easier. Because most of the users are middle-aged people who are not very familiar with smartphones, the style of this design is lucid and intuitive.

This version of the application is available in App Store.
